Component C08

Coverage

The "over thirty contracted sources" section on Home.

What it is

Home section that backs the "over thirty contracted sources" claim and links to Notices.

Print

FrameFull width less 32 each side (20 on phone). 640 tall, overflow hidden. City photo treated in print: grayscale, contrast 1.65, red screen overlay (multiply in dark), −45° hatch 3 / 5, and a grain layer.
CardAnchored bottom right, 520 wide, padding 40 44 44, --bg fill. Eyebrow "Coverage", H2 clamp(34, 3.6vw, 48), text 17 / 26, link "Browse notices".
PhoneFrame grows to min-height 560, auto height. Card sits in flow with margin-top 300, full width, so the photo shows above it.

Night

FrameSection on the dot-field ground (dots 14 px grid, 1.2 px, --dot), padding 112 0 (72 phone). Two columns, gap 64, centred. One column at ≤ 960, gap 40.
TextMono label "Coverage", H2 Inter Tight 700 clamp(36, 4vw, 52), body 18 / 1.55 --body max 520, link "Browse notices".
Source tilesIn a --surf card (fill --bg, padding 28): 32 tiles, 8 columns, gap 10 (6 on phone). Tile 1:1, 1px #EF4136 border, --tile fill, radius 3. Caption "Each tile is a contracted source. Source names: [LIST FROM KLARUM]".

Content

Claim"Over thirty contracted sources". Print and Night word it the same. The Night tile count (32) is illustrative, not a source count.
To supply[Source names list]. City photo is the one in the source (webp, 205 kB).

States

In sourceStatic. Link hover per C02.
MissingNone.

Decide

EvidenceTiles are anonymous. Replace with source logos or names once supplied: [list].
PhotoConfirm the city photo is licensed for the live site.
