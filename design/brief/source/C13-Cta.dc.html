Component C13

Closing call to action

The last push before the footer: one headline and the Start free button.

What it is

The closing call to action before the footer. One headline, one primary action.

Print

FramePadding 32 0. A striped photo band above (190 tall) and a mirrored one below (100 tall), both inset 32 left and right (20 on phone).
Headline"Source more." Plex Sans 400, clamp(52, 12vw, 168), line-height .95, tracking −.055em, riso filter.
RowLead 20 / 30, max 560, left. Large primary button right: 60 tall, padding 0 30, 18 px, arrow. Row wraps on phone.
PagesHome and Pricing.

Night

FrameFull width, 1px --line on top, overflow hidden. Field image cover at 70% 75%, in light mode inverted (invert, hue-rotate 180, saturate 1.5).
ScrimLeft to right: rgb(--scrim) at .92, .55 (45%), .1 (75%).
ContentPadding 120 0 (72 on phone). Headline Inter Tight 700, clamp(40, 5vw, 64), max 760. Buttons gap 16: primary Start free, secondary See pricing, link "View whole demo as a guest".
HeadlinesHome: "Stop searching. Start with the work that fits." Notices and Pricing: "See it on your own pipeline."

Phone (≤ 760)

PrintHeadline 52 px. Padding 20 at the sides. Row stacks.
NightHeadline 40 px (source drops to clamp minimum), padding 72, buttons wrap.

States

In sourceDefault. Buttons hover per C02.
MissingNone needed beyond focus. Proposed as C02.

Decide

HeadlinePrint says "Source more.", Night says a full sentence. Pick one voice.
Guest demo link"View whole demo as a guest" exists in Night only. Is there a guest demo: [yes/no].
Second actionPrint has one action here, Night has three. One primary is the rule, so drop the link unless the demo exists.
