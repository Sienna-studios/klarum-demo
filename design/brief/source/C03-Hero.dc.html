Component C03

Hero and proof strip

First screen of Home. Says what Klarum is, offers Start free, links to Pricing, then a strip for logos of the people behind it.

Print

FrameFull-bleed striped photo band (#1d2931 base, center 38%, cover). Padding top clamp(96, 16vw, 200), bottom clamp(72, 12vw, 150). A solid --bg panel sits inside it.
Grid12 columns, gap 24, panel padding 40 top, 44 bottom. H1 spans 1 to 8, side column spans 9 to 12 with a 1px --rule on its left and padding 44 0 0 24.
HeadlinePlex Sans 400. Line one 22 / 30. Line two clamp(40, 7vw, 96), line-height .96, tracking −.045em, riso filter, set as "Infrastructure / Procurement."
SideEyebrow (Plex Mono 12, .14em, --redt) · lead 17 / 26 · primary button + text link, gap 20.
Tag chipsTwo chips pinned 22 from the band bottom: URL left, address right. #1d2931 on #fbfdf6, 13 px, padding 4 8.

Night

Frame16:9, min-height 640, overflow hidden. Field image (dark or light) cover, plus a left-to-right scrim: rgb(--scrim) at .88, .45 (42%), 0 (62%).
CopyAbsolute, left 64, top 128, width 620. Mono label, then H1 Inter Tight 800 at 80 / .98 / −.04em, lead 19 / 1.5 max 480 in --body, buttons gap 20.
Map layerThree pins (14, 3px ring) and notice cards 244 wide with fit meter. Positions are percentages of the frame. Cards open to the right of the pin, the third to the left.

Proof strip

LayoutRow under the hero, 1px top rule, padding 36 0. Mono caption "Built and backed by people from", then 5 logo slots in an auto-fit grid, min 130, gap 16.
SlotDashed 1.5px --line (--off in Night), 52 tall in Print, 56 tall and radius 4 in Night. Label [LOGO] until real marks are supplied.

Phone (≤ 760)

PrintPanel stacks, side column becomes a top-ruled block under the H1 with margin 28. Padding 28 / 32.
NightPins and cards are hidden, scrim hidden. Copy goes in flow with padding 56 20 0, H1 44 px. Frame grows padding-bottom clamp(260, 50vw, 420) so the field image shows below, anchored 70% 100%.

States

In sourceStatic. Buttons follow C02.
Loading, errorNone. The hero has no data. Not needed.
MotionThe logo-hero boards (right, below) add a 10 s resolve loop with a reduced-motion fallback. Not in either site page.

Decide

CopyPrint and Night write different headlines. Pick one: "The Intelligent System of Record for Infrastructure Procurement." or "Source more of the work that fits."
Logos[Five logos or a claim to replace the placeholders.]
