Who it serves

Primary

Infrastructure bid teams

Both directions name them in the eyebrow: For infrastructure bid teams (Print) and Procurement software for infrastructure bid teams (Night). Sample notices are rail, bridges, tunnels, foundations, ground, water and roads.

Who pays

The firm, per seat

Free is one person per organisation, Business adds team seats, Partner is negotiated for firms. So the reader of Pricing is often a bid manager or director deciding for a team, not the person who found the site.

Later

Issuers

Night only: Portal · for issuers. In development. It sits in the main nav today. Decide whether an unfinished product earns a top-level nav item [decision].
