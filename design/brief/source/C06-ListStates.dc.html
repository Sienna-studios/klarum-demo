Notice list and states

The results column: count line, rows, and the empty, loading and error cases.

What it is

The results column on Notices: a count line, the rows from C04, and what shows when there is nothing to list.

HeaderPlex Mono caps 12, --mute. Left "N of 8 notices · from [31] sources", right "Sorted by fit". Padding-bottom 16.
ListRows with a hairline between them; one closing hairline under the last.
EmptyPadding 48, 1.5px dashed --line, centred, --mute. Copy: "No notices match. Clear the search or widen the filters."

Night

HeaderJetBrains Mono caps 12, --mute. "N of 8 notices · [31] sources" left, "Sorted by fit" right. Padding-bottom 14.
ListAll rows inside one --surf card (radius 6, overflow hidden).
EmptySame copy, padding 40, centred, --mute, inside the card.

States

In sourceDefault, filtered (type, fit, search), shortlisted, empty.
ProposedLoading: three skeleton rows in --line / --off, static. Error: single message with a "Try again" link. Neither exists in the boards; copy is a suggestion.
ShortlistToggling a row moves nothing; only the row style and the panel count change (C05).

Phone (≤ 760)

BothOne column; rows use the phone layout in C04. Filters sit above and no longer stick.
