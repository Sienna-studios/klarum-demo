Component C09

Direction-only sections

Sections only one direction has: Print wiring; Night statement, lens, registry and portal.

What it is

Sections that exist in one direction only. Print has the wiring diagram. Night has the statement, the capability lens, the registry and the issuer strip. Each is a candidate to carry into the chosen direction.

Decide

WiringKeep the diagram if the page needs a multi-party story. It is the most distinctive Print section.
Lens and RegistryBoth say "your capability index" twice. Keep one or merge into one section.
PortalListed in nav and footer while In development. Keep the strip, drop the nav item.

States

In sourceWiring: five party states. Lens: static or pulsing. Others static.
MissingFocus ring on party buttons exists in Print (2px #ef4136, offset 2).
