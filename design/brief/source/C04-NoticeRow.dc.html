Component C04

Notice row

One notice in the feed: what it is, how well it fits, its revision, and a shortlist toggle.

Print

GridColumns: title block, fit, revision tag, shortlist button. Gap 24, padding 20 0, 1px --line rule on top.
Title blockMeta line Plex Mono 11 caps: type · category · Closes [date]. Title 24 / 30 / 500, −.01em. Under it 14 --mute: [Buyer] · [Value] · [Region].
FitFive squares, 12 px, gap 4. Filled --red = fit; empty = 1.5px --line outline.
Revision tag26 tall, padding 0 8, 1px --red border, Plex Mono 11 / 500, .1em, --redt. Hover or focus opens a 280 px --ink popover with the history, .16s.
Shortlist44 tall, padding 0 16, 1.5px --ink border, 14 / 500. Pressed: #ef4136 fill, #141b21 text.
ShortlistedTitle gets a 2px red underline, offset 6. Fit squares switch to −45° hatch (red 2px, gap 3) with a red outline.

Night

RowInside a --surf card, radius 6, 1px --line. Row grid: title, pips, button; gap 8 20; padding 16 20; hairline between rows.
TextTitle Inter 16 / 600 / 1.35. Meta in JetBrains Mono 11 caps, two lines: type · category · [Buyer], then Closes [date].
FitFive pips, 7 px, gap 3. #EF4136 = fit; --off = empty.
Shortlist40 tall, padding 0 14, radius 4, 1px --off, 13 / 600. Pressed: #EF4136 fill, #0D0D10 text. Row tint --tile.
Compact rowHome feed preview: title 14 / 600, padding 12 16, pips right, two mono lines.

Phone (≤ 760)

PrintTwo columns. Fit squares sit under the title, rev tag and button fill the right column.
NightTitle and pips on row one. Meta lines full width. Shortlist button full row, left aligned.

States

In sourceDefault, shortlisted, revision popover (Print).
Not in sourceHover on the row itself. Row hover is not defined in either direction. Proposed none needed; the button carries the action.

Decide

Fit encodingSquares with hatch (Print) or pips with a row tint (Night).
Revision tagPrint only. Night rows carry no revision. Keep it if the notice history is a product feature: [yes/no].
Shortlist targetNight button is 40 tall. Raise it to 44 to meet the touch-target rule in the layout tokens.
