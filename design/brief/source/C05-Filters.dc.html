Behaviour

FilteringType, fit and search combine. Search matches title and category. Fit chip keeps rows with 4 or more.
CountResults header shows "N of 8 notices · from [31] sources" (Print) and "· [31] sources" (Night). Sorted by fit.
Phone (≤ 760)Column goes full width, stops sticking, sits above the results.
