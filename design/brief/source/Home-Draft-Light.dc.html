Questions

Before you start.

Is the free tier a trial?

No. The free tier is the floor of the plan ladder and does not expire. It covers one person per organisation with capped matches each month.

How does the Business trial work?

Start a 7-day trial of Business, or subscribe straight away. [Answer: what happens when the trial ends]

How are seats billed?

Business is 200 € per seat per month, excluding VAT. Add or remove seats any time; changes are prorated by Stripe. A team of 4 is 800 € a month.

What does Partner change?

Seat price, term, payment days, seat count and billing interval are set by agreement, invoiced against an agreement reference. An optional development fee covers bespoke integrations.

Which sources are covered?

Over thirty contracted sources, from public-sector portals to development-bank procurement. Need a connector or market we do not cover yet? [See integrations]
