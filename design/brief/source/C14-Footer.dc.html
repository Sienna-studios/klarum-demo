Component C14

Footer

The last block on every page. Print uses a drawing-sheet title block; Night uses a big K mark.

What it is

Site footer. Print ends the page like a drawing sheet with a title block. Night ends with navigation and a very large K mark cut off at the bottom.

Print

Ground--foot: #141b21 in light, #0d1216 in dark. Text #f3f2ec, secondary #aeb6bc, rules #2a343c. Links #d9dcd6, #fff on hover.
Grid12 columns, gap 24, padding 64 0 40. Brand + line spans 1 to 4, Product 5 to 6, Read 7 to 8, title block 9 to 12.
BrandLogo 28 tall, inverted. Line "The Intelligent System of Record for Infrastructure Procurement." 15 / 23, max 320.
NavEyebrow in red, then links 15 px, gap 10. Product: Notices, Pricing, sign in. Read: Articles, Privacy, Terms.
Title blockTable with 1px #2a343c cells, padding 8 12. Keys Plex Mono 10 caps. Cells: logo (2 wide, 2 tall), Sheet A-102, Rev F (red), Title, Scale 1:1, Office, Web klarum.com, Year 2026.
Bottom bar1px rule, padding 20 0 28, 13 px. URL (underlined) left, address right.

Night

GroundPage --bg, 1px --line on top, overflow hidden.
Grid1.4fr 1fr 1fr 1fr, gap 48, padding 72 0 300 (room for the mark).
BrandRed K mark 37.8 × 28 next to "Klarum" Inter 28 bold. Line 14 / 1.5 --sub, max 300.
NavMono label, then links 15 px, gap 12. Product: Notices, Registry, Portal, Pricing. Company: About, Careers, Ask us directly. Legal: Privacy, Terms, Security.
MarkK mark in --surf, width min(1180, 120%), height 872, centred, bottom −260, behind the links.
Legal line"© 2026 Klarum", mono 11, 24 from the bottom.

Phone (≤ 760)

PrintEvery block goes full width with 28 between. Links and the bottom-bar URL get 44 tall targets. Bottom bar stacks.
NightGrid is 2 columns at ≤ 960, 1 at ≤ 760. Bottom padding 200. Links 44 tall, gap 0.

In sourceDefault and link hover (Print #fff, Night --redt).
Not in sourceFocus ring. Proposed 2px #ef4136, offset 2.
