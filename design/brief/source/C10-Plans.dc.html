Component C10

Pricing plans

Plan cards and the billing toggle. Business is the recommended plan.

What it is

Three plan cards (Free, Business, Partner) and a Monthly / Yearly toggle on the Pricing page.

Print

Grid3 equal columns, gap 24, align start. One column at ≤ 960.
CardPadding 32, 1px --line border, --card fill. Name as eyebrow, one-line description 15 px, price 52 px / −.04em, unit line in Plex Mono 11 caps.
RecommendedBusiness: 2.5px --red outline at offset 10 (6 on phone) and a red tag above the card, "REV A · FOR TEAMS", Plex Mono 11, left −12, top −38.
ButtonsFree: ghost "Start free". Business: red "Start 7-day trial" plus "or subscribe now" link. Partner: ghost "Book a call". All full width, 48 tall.
ListsLabel (mono 11 caps, 1px rule above), then 15 / 1.4 items, gap 12. Included = hatched square 14. Not included = 14 × 1.5 dash, --mute. "Planned" chip for SSO / SCIM.

Night

Card--surf, radius 6, padding 32. Price Inter Tight 800, 48 px. Unit line JetBrains Mono 11 caps.
Recommended1.5px #EF4136 border plus a 4px --tile halo. "For teams" chip (ink on bg, mono 10 caps) in the header row.
ButtonsFree and Partner secondary, Business primary red. Full width.
Lists8 px red squares. Not included = 2 px dash in --off, text --mute. "Planned" chip with 1px --off border.

Toggle

ControlSegmented, 3px inset, buttons 40 tall. Pressed = --ink fill, --bg text. "−17%" in red.
EffectOnly Business changes: 200 € → 166 € per seat, unit line gains "billed yearly", team line 800 → 664 € / month.

Copy facts

Business200 € per seat per month, 166 € yearly (−17%), 7-day trial, excl. VAT, prorated by Stripe.
Free0 €, no expiry, one person per org.
PartnerSet by agreement, invoiced.

Phone (≤ 760)

BothCards stack, padding 28 24, min-height 0. Toggle moves under the heading.
