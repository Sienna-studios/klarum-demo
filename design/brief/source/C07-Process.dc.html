Component C07

How it works

Three steps from notice to signed contract.

What it is

Home section that explains the product in three steps: Source, Bid, Award. Print runs it as a stepper with a live panel. Night runs it as three cards.

Print stepper

Layout4fr stepper, 8fr panel, gap 56. One column at ≤ 960, gap 36.
StepRow min-height 84: numbered circle (34, 1.5px --red), name 26 / −.02em, mono sub-label. Active: --ink text, 2px red line under the row, circle filled red.
Steps1 Source · Gather, fit. 2 Bid · Checklist, assemble. 3 Award · Signed.
CaptionEyebrow "N · Name · line", then 19 / 30 sentence. Copy per step is in the preview.

Print panels

ShellPadding 32 (20 on phone), 1px --line, --card fill.
SourceRequirement rows: requirement, status (Match / No match), capability. A gap row has a red border. Six segments show fit; "Fit 5 of 6 · Go" at 22 / 500.
BidChecklist rows: checkbox 18, item, owner circle 28, state. State column hides on phone. Below: a quoted passage with a revision-style citation tag.
AwardRed block (#ef4136, text #141b21), padding 44 (28 phone). "Awarded." Plex Sans clamp(64, 9vw, 120), riso. Three keyed values, each with a 2px rule: Contract value, Sourced, Bid assembled in.

Print desktop

NoteOn a wide screen the source board plays this as a scroll-driven sequence 4860 px tall, pinned at 810 (gather, fit, checklist, award takeover). The stepper above is the layout used on small screens and is the static reference.

Night cards

LayoutThree equal cards in a grid, gap 24. Each is --surf, radius 6, overflow hidden. One column at ≤ 960.
Top150 tall, hairline below. Red K mark cropped through a mask at 330% size, top left. Label chip top left: "01 Sourcing", "02 Decision and drafting", "03 Lifecycle" (mono 12 / 600, --redt, on --bg).
BodyPadding 24 28 20. Title 22 / 1.25 / 600, text 15 / 1.55 --sub.
Bottom01: three compact notice rows. 02: draft excerpt with red left rule and citation tag. 03: four stages as rows of 9 px squares (39, 14, 9, 4) plus three KPI placeholders [ ].

States

In sourcePrint: three steps, hover and click. Night: static.
Not in sourceStep focus ring. Proposed 2px #ef4136 outline, offset 2.

Decide

VoicesPrint explains by showing one tender end to end. Night shows three product areas. They answer different questions: choose one.
Placeholders[Company], [14] projects, [9] bridges, [X], [Value], [Date], [X] days and the KPI values need real or clearly fake data.
